# Change Orders and Change Requests Discussed in Transcripts

Review date: 2026-09-21. Evidence period: 2026-08-26 through 2026-09-17.

This register covers **all 19 transcript files in the repository, representing seven unique meetings**. Identical copies filed under different applications were compared by SHA-256 and counted once. It includes commercial scope changes, requested enhancements, process changes, and material alternatives even where they were deferred or declined.

**No formally authorized change order, signed change approval, or agreed change-order price was identified in the transcripts.** One discussion explicitly says a change order is likely; several others identify out-of-scope work or additional expenditure. Agreement on a design is distinguished from authorization to charge for a change.

The `CR-` identifiers below are local register references, not customer-issued change-request or change-order numbers. Repeated discussions of the same outcome are consolidated. Alternative implementations are recorded together where they address the same request.

## How to read the status

- **Accepted design:** supported by an accepted repository ADR; commercial approval is not implied.
- **Agreed in discussion:** participants expressed a direction or commitment, but formal approval or delivery was not established.
- **Proposed / open:** the request needs a decision, investigation, detailed design, or scope confirmation.
- **Discussed / future:** an option or future use case, without a commitment to implement it.
- **Declined / superseded:** considered but not adopted, or replaced by a later direction.
- **Already scoped / standard:** retained for completeness; the evidence does not establish an incremental commercial change.

The latest ADR status is used when reconciling current design. An ADR marked proposed remains proposed even where the workshop language sounds positive. Some ADRs have later revisions than the available transcripts; these are identified as current design context, not evidence of another transcript or commercial approval. Workshop speaker labels are unreliable, so they have not been used to assign requesters or approvers.

## 7. Items to take into a commercial scope review first

1. **CR-001 and CR-002:** the clearest explicit additional-scope opportunities—digital assessment forms and the offboarding reassignment package.
2. **CR-004, CR-005 and CR-006:** inspection target tracking, SPCC consolidation and equipment filtering need a precise included/excluded boundary before estimation.
3. **CR-028–031:** reconcile the standalone qualification design, sequencing, renewal and validity rules against the actual contracted competency deliverable. The transcripts contain a material challenge to the SOW's expiry logic, without an approved commercial resolution.
4. **CR-015, CR-018–026, CR-036, CR-041–043 and CR-066:** confirm whether these enhancements, integrations and reports fit existing implementation allowances. Their appearance in a workshop does not establish that they are chargeable changes.
5. Retain declined and deferred items, particularly **CR-003, CR-007, CR-013–014 and CR-044**, so they are not accidentally reinstated as current requirements.

For any formal CO, the remaining evidence needed is the applicable SOW baseline, agreed delta, estimate, commercial approver and written authorization. None should be inferred from an accepted ADR alone.
