# Establish First-Year Platform Change Governance

## Context and Problem Statement

After go-live, users will request new access, reports, fields, workflows, and configuration changes. Those requests can affect security, licensing, maintainability, and several applications. Intelex does not supply the organizational decision process needed to assess them.

## Decision Drivers

* Security and cross-application impact review.
* Limited administrative and report-authoring capacity.
* Transparent prioritization.
* Sustainable platform ownership after implementation.

## Considered Options

* Allow administrators to implement requests as they arrive.
* Route every request through a large formal committee.
* Establish a proportionate intake, triage, prioritization, and approval forum.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **Establish a proportionate intake, triage, prioritization, and approval forum for the first year.**

The operating model will review business rationale, security, licensing, and architectural impact; maintain a shared work pipeline or roadmap; assign implementation responsibility; and meet at a sustainable cadence. Jeremiah Soto will be included in defining the model.

Committee membership, cadence, product-owner roles, and approval thresholds remain unresolved.

## Consequences

* Changes can be evaluated consistently before implementation.
* Competing requests can be prioritized transparently.
* Governance adds process and may slow minor changes.
* An oversized or poorly owned forum could become ineffective.
