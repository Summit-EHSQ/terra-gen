# Report on Deficiencies, Completion, Timeliness, and Trends

## Context and Problem Statement

The inspection program is intended to show strengths, weaknesses, and improvement over time. Useful performance information comes from deficient responses, findings, corrective actions, completion against frequency requirements, and timeliness rather than only aggregate checklist scores.

## Decision Drivers

* The organization needs to identify trends and improvement opportunities.
* Deficiencies derive from configured negative responses.
* Completion must be compared with site-level requirements.
* Late completion may differ materially from on-time completion.
* Significant findings may need grouping by severity or category.

## Considered Options

* Report primarily on numeric scores.
* Report only the number of completed inspections.
* Report on deficiencies, findings, actions, compliance, timeliness, and trends.
* Produce reports manually from PDF records.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **Report on deficiencies, findings, actions, completion, timeliness, and trends**, because these measures reflect the stated objectives more directly than numeric scores. Analysis will support relevant dimensions such as OSHA establishment, operational resource or site, technology, program, period, category, and severity where available.

Establishment and resource are separate reporting dimensions. The organizational hierarchy supplies establishment aggregation, while the selected operational resource provides the more granular location and technology context.

## Consequences

* Stakeholders can monitor inspection activity and resulting issues.
* Site compliance can be displayed against configured requirements.
* Consistent answer semantics and classifications are essential.
* Both direct-action and finding-based paths must be represented.
* Detailed KPI definitions and dashboard layouts remain to be established.
* Resource and establishment master data must remain consistently related.

## More Information

Candidate measures include deficiency counts, completion percentage, overdue completion, findings by severity, action status, and trends by site. A final KPI catalogue was not approved in the meeting.
