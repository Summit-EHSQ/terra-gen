# Solution Brief: Operational Activity Targets

Operational Activity Targets is a proposed Intelex application for tracking whether required operational work is being completed at the right frequency. Its initial use is to monitor qualifying inspections—for example, confirming that each site or operational resource completes at least two required inspections per year. The design is reusable, so it could later track other activities such as hazard identifications, near-miss reports, or safety-engagement hours. Importantly, it tracks requirements separately from the activities themselves, allowing the application to show missed targets and periods with no activity without creating placeholder inspection records.

The application has four main components. **Source Measures** define what activity should be counted or totalled and which conditions it must meet. For inspections, these conditions could include inspection type, checklist, completion status, outcome, location, or operational resource. **Target Rules and Scope Assignments** define the required amount, reporting frequency, whether the target is a minimum or maximum, and where it applies. Targets may apply across the enterprise, to a location, or to a specific operational resource. **Target Periods** represent each reporting obligation, such as a calendar year or quarter. **Target Activities and Contributions** evaluate completed source records and connect qualifying results to the appropriate target periods.

Configuration administrators create and publish a Source Measure. Publishing preserves a locked version of the measure and its rules so that historical results can always be traced to the configuration used at the time. Target administrators then define the target and assign it to the appropriate locations or resources, including responsible and escalation workgroups.

Automated services create the required reporting periods, evaluate new or changed inspections, and reconcile qualifying activity against each target. For a count-based measure, each qualifying inspection contributes one. For a sum-based measure, it contributes a numeric value such as hours. The application calculates the current total and reports each period as **Future, On Track, At Risk, Met, or Missed**. It also records calculation health so users can distinguish genuine non-compliance from incomplete or failed processing.

Managers and analysts use dashboards and reports to review performance, identify locations at risk, and drill into the activities contributing to a result. Native Intelex reminders can notify responsible workgroups as deadlines approach. Business users can review results but cannot change system-calculated periods or contributions, while existing source-record permissions continue to protect sensitive inspection information.
